2 · Frequently asked questions

Does MarginBook use the network? Where does my data go?▾

Not while you work. There is no network request anywhere in the App’s code — recording entries, viewing reports, calculating break-even and exporting all happen on this device.

Only three App Store actions go online: fetching the product and its price when you open the Settings screen, starting a purchase when you tap “Unlock full version”, and checking your receipt in the store sandbox when you tap “Restore purchase”. All three are handled by Apple’s system frameworks — we never see your Apple ID, your payment method or your bill.

Your ledger has never left this device.

Do I need to sign up?▾

No — and no email, no phone number, no identifying information of any kind. Download it and go; close it and open it again later, and it is the same ledger.

There is no login, no cloud account, and no step that says “sign up before you can use it”.

Where is my data stored? Can other Apps see it?▾

It is in the private sandbox iOS gives this App. The ledger, the channels, the categories and the settings all live inside that one container.

No other App has permission to read what is in here, and MarginBook has no channel that uploads it — so “another App sees my numbers” is not something that can happen with this App.

I am switching phones — how do I take my data with me?▾

MarginBook does no cloud sync, so there is no automatic path between two devices. Two ways:

① Export CSV: Settings › Data › Export data › Export CSV (always free). Save the file in the Files app or send it to yourself. Note that CSV is a format for you and your accountant to read, not an import format for the App — after you switch, the next route is the one you want.

② Use the iOS device backup: an iCloud backup or a backup to a connected computer migrates the whole App data, provided your backup includes this App.

Will uninstalling the App delete my ledger?▾

Yes. When iOS removes an App it removes its sandbox container too.

That is the price of “your data stays on this device”, and we are stating it here rather than hiding it. If the numbers matter, export a CSV first and keep a copy.

I have iCloud backup on — does that count as MarginBook uploading to the cloud?▾

No. That is the operating system’s device backup: with iCloud backup on, iOS encrypts and backs up the files in the App’s sandbox to Apple together with every other App’s data.

MarginBook has no server, no backup service and no background sync task — we cannot see that backup, and there is no other way in to it.

What can the free version do? What does the one-time unlock add?▾

Three things are free forever: recording entries (no limit on how many), CSV export, and break-even plus gross and net profit with history. None of these disappear because you did not buy the unlock.

The unlock adds three things: multi-sheet Excel export, a one-page monthly business report as PDF, and custom payment channels and categories (only after the unlock does the whole “Settings › Bookkeeping › Payment channels / Categories” group appear).

The unlock is one-time, not a subscription. It never expires, and it is never taken back because development stopped or the price went up. The price shown in the App Store varies by region.

I already bought it — why does the new phone still say locked?▾

Tap Settings › Unlock full version › Restore purchase. The status there becomes Unlocked · thank you.

The receipt for a non-consumable lives in the store sandbox, so if you do not restore it the purchase is as if it never happened — restoring checks it online once, and that is exactly why we put “Restore purchase” where it is easy to find in Settings.

Why can break-even sometimes not be calculated?▾

It is not broken — it genuinely cannot be computed. The App tells you which one it is instead of inventing a number for you. Four cases:

① No entries this month yet — log a few first;
② No income recorded yet — with no money received there is no break-even to talk about;
③ Gross profit is negative — selling more loses more right now, so sort out the cost structure first;
④ No fixed costs recorded — stall fee, rent and wages have to be logged as “Fixed costs” so that break-even has something to divide by.

Once it can be computed, the home screen shows the number rounded up to the major unit (for example ¥362.77 is shown as ¥363.00), and the gap is measured against that rounded line — so the difference you see is always a difference you can actually close.

Who is each of the three export files for?▾

CSV — a detail sheet, one row per entry, for your accountant or for you to read through. Always free.

Excel (.xlsx) — 5 sheets: detail, categories, channels, monthly and notes. The amounts are real numbers you can sum directly, so hand it to whoever continues working on the spreadsheet. Unlocked by purchase.

Monthly business report (.pdf) — one page: what you made this month, the minimum you must sell each day, and your three biggest costs — for your own review, or for your boss or family. Unlocked by purchase.

I sent an exported file to someone — can you see it?▾

No. The export writes a file into the App’s temporary directory and opens the system share sheet; every step after that happens between you and the recipient.

We have no server, and no endpoint that receives files.

Will the App ask me for camera, photo or location permissions?▾

No. There is not a single permission usage description in the App’s Info.plist, and the code has no camera, album or location calls.

The only system prompt you will ever see is Apple’s purchase confirmation sheet, and the share sheet when you export.

Which languages and currencies are supported?▾

The interface supports Simplified Chinese, English, 日本語 and 한국어. You can switch at any time in Settings › General › Language, and the change takes effect immediately.

The currency follows your storefront market by default, but you can lock it yourself (Settings › General › Currency). “Market” is a separate setting: your channels and cost structure follow where you do business, not the language of the interface — you may be running an English interface while accounting for a European or American market.

Can I request a feature or report a bug?▾

Yes. Write to us — the address is at the bottom of the page.

Please include: your device model and OS version, the App version, what you were doing at the time, and a screenshot of the screen if you have one. But do not send us your ledger — reproducing a problem never requires your books.
